## Queue

### A. pr9 and sgpu1: fork, hold, kernel — DONE 2026-11-17

The router now lets `100.64.65.111` and `.112` out. pr9 moved from the
enterprise repositories to `pve-no-subscription`. Both were upgraded, got the
fork in the same apt transaction as the kept-back packages, were held, and
were rebooted one at a time into 7.0.14-29, with quorum kept throughout.
Their chrony still lists the AD domain controllers next to the public pool.

### B. pr9 and sgpu1: networking — DONE 2026-11-17

Management on copper without a bridge, `vmbr0` on 10G port 1, VLAN 1098 tagged
on 10G port 2 (`.111`/`.112`). Moved to [`docs/cluster.md`](../docs/cluster.md),
*Bridges*. Still open:
- neither host has a `vmbr1` yet;
- the switch should give both trunks the same unused native VLAN;
- the PBS bond is LACP, but the switch does not form the port-channel, so the
  PBS runs on one 10G link.

### C. spx1: both RAID 50 volumes degraded — NEW, URGENT, hardware

Found on 2026-11-17:

- **Storage `ssd`:** on the internal Smart Array P420i, the SSD in bay 7 failed.
- **Storage `exthd`:** in the MD1200 on the PERC H810, the disk in slot 4 left
  the array at the 2026-11-07 reboot. That disk also has 246 reallocated sectors.

Each volume has one parity group running without redundancy. Replace the two
disks and let the controllers rebuild. `ssacli` and `perccli` are now
installed on spx1.
