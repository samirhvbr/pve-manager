## Queue

### A. pr9 and sgpu1: fork, hold, kernel — DONE 2026-11-17

The router now lets `100.64.65.111` and `.112` out. pr9 moved from the
enterprise repositories to `pve-no-subscription`. Both were upgraded, got the
fork in the same apt transaction as the kept-back packages, were held, and
were rebooted one at a time into 7.0.14-29, with quorum kept throughout.
Their chrony still lists the AD domain controllers next to the public pool.

### B. pr9 and sgpu1: networking — NEW

The plan for both hosts:

- **10G port 1:** guests;
- **10G port 2:** VLAN 1098, the network to the PBS;
- **copper port:** management.

Today both 10G ports are down, with breakout cables, and `vmbr0` sits on the
copper port. Once the ports have link:

- move `vmbr0` to 10G port 1, so guest configs and bridge ACLs work as on the
  other nodes;
- keep the management IP on copper;
- add the VLAN 1098 interface;
- create `vmbr1` on a port with no link.

Addresses on VLAN 1098 (`100.64.98.0/24`): `.1` is the router and `.2` is the
PBS. The plan is to reuse each host's last management octet: `.111` sgpu1,
`.112` pr9, and `.160`/`.202` reserved for b3p1/spx1. These were free by ARP
on 2026-11-17. Until then, backups from the new hosts reach the PBS at
`100.64.98.2` through the router, like b3p1 and spx1 do. Do not configure the
1098 address on a port with no link, because Linux would keep routing to the
PBS through the dead port.

On 2026-11-17 all four 10G ports (pr9 `nic6`/`nic7`, sgpu1 `nic4`/`enp68s0f1`)
had no link even when brought up. All four ends report the same breakout cable
serial.

### C. spx1: both RAID 50 volumes degraded — NEW, URGENT, hardware

Found on 2026-11-17:

- **Storage `ssd`:** on the internal Smart Array P420i, the SSD in bay 7 failed.
- **Storage `exthd`:** in the MD1200 on the PERC H810, the disk in slot 4 left
  the array at the 2026-11-07 reboot. That disk also has 246 reallocated sectors.

Each volume has one parity group running without redundancy. Replace the two
disks and let the controllers rebuild. `ssacli` and `perccli` are now
installed on spx1.
